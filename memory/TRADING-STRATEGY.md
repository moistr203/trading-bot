# Trading Strategy

Swing trading US stocks on catalysts. Hold days to weeks, not minutes.
Buy strong stocks in strong sectors when there is a specific, fresh reason (news, earnings beat,
upgrade, big contract). Protect every position with a trailing stop. Cut losers fast at -7%,
let winners run with a tightening trail. Fewer, better trades: max 3 new buys a week.

The hard rules live in CLAUDE.md and scripts/alpaca.py. This file only records lessons.
