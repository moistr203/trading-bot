# Trading Strategy

Swing trading US stocks on catalysts. Hold days to weeks, not minutes.
Buy strong stocks in strong sectors when there is a specific, fresh reason (news, earnings beat,
upgrade, big contract). Protect every position with a trailing stop. Cut losers fast at -7%,
let winners run with a tightening trail. Fewer, better trades: max 3 new buys a week.

The hard rules live in CLAUDE.md and scripts/alpaca.py. This file only records lessons.

## Lessons
(The Friday weekly review appends dated lessons here.)

### 2026-11-27
- Cash is a valid position. In a short holiday week the checklist blocked chases and weak-sector buys; that is the system working.
- A catalyst ages fast. If a good idea is blocked only for chasing, re-check it within a day or two for a calmer entry, while the news is still fresh.
- When a sector is below its 20-day average, hunt in sectors that are trending up instead of forcing names from the weak one.
